Privacy

Skyport Dynasty · last updated 22 October 2026

Skyport Dynasty does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-ins or ads.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • The game makes no network connections of its own.
  • Your saved games stay on your device. If you turn on iCloud for the game, they are kept in your own iCloud account, which only you can reach.

Questions? See the support page.
