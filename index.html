Support

Skyport Dynasty

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/brettthomson/skyport-dynasty-site/issues. It needs a free GitHub account.

Please include your iPhone model, the game's tier and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Where are my saved games? On your device (or in your iCloud, if turned on). Deleting the game deletes saves that aren't in iCloud.

Does the game collect data? No. See the privacy page.
